Store lifecycle: staged -> active -> retiring -> remove.
Provision the referenced Store credential Secrets before enabling workloads.

Use --wait --timeout 15m for upgrades; then wait for terminating Gateway Pods too.
Helm template/GitOps, rollback, uninstall and direct Kubernetes edits do not enforce
live lifecycle checks. Follow the operational runbook:
